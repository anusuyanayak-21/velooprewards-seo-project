## How Referral Rewards Work

A referral program rewards you for bringing someone new to a platform. The platform gains a new user, the person who invited gets a benefit, and sometimes the new user gets a welcome benefit too.

## The basic steps

1. **You get a referral link or code.** It is unique to your account.
2. **You share it with someone.** Friends, family or your community.
3. **They sign up using it.** The platform links their account to yours.
4. **A condition is met.** This may be sign-up, verification or first activity.
5. **A reward is credited.** The reward amount and timing follow the platform's rules.

## What counts as a valid referral?

Rules differ, but common conditions include:

- The new person must be a genuinely new user.
- They must use your code or link at sign-up.
- They may need to verify their account or complete an action.
- Self-referrals and duplicate accounts are normally not allowed.

## One level or multiple levels?

Some programs reward only direct referrals. Others also reward activity from people your referrals invite. Always read the programme rules. [CONFIRM VELoop structure.]

**Example 1 (generic):** You invite a friend. They sign up with your code and complete a first task. After that task is verified, your account receives a referral reward.

**Example 2 (generic):** A platform gives a welcome reward to the new user and a separate reward to the inviter.

## How to share a referral link responsibly

- Be honest about what the platform is and what it offers.
- Do not promise income or guaranteed rewards.
- Never spam groups or strangers.
- Tell people to read the rules themselves.

## Referral red flags

- A programme that asks you to pay to earn referral rewards.
- Promises of unlimited or guaranteed income.
- Unclear rules for when rewards are paid.

## How Reward Platforms Work

A reward platform is a website or app that gives you something back for taking part in activities. The activity might be watching a short ad, finishing a task, inviting a friend, playing a game or logging in each day. The reward might be points, tokens or other platform credits that you can later use in the ways the platform allows.

This guide explains the basic mechanics so you know what to expect and what to check before you sign up.

## Why do platforms offer rewards?

Platforms reward activity because the activity has value to them. An advertiser pays to reach viewers, a company wants feedback, or a platform wants an active community. Rewards share a part of that value with users. This is also why rewards are usually small and depend on the platform's own rules.

## The basic flow

1. **Create an account.** You sign up with details the platform asks for.
2. **Take part in activities.** Each activity has its own rules, limits and reward.
3. **Rewards are recorded.** They appear as a balance, often in a wallet.
4. **You redeem or withdraw.** Redemption options and conditions vary by platform.

## Common ways to earn

### Watching ads
You view ads for a set time and receive a reward. Platforms often limit how many you can watch per day.

### Completing tasks
Tasks can be simple actions such as visiting a page, following instructions or answering questions. Rewards depend on the task.

### Referrals
You share an invitation link or code. When someone joins under the platform's conditions, you may receive a reward. See our guide on [how referral rewards work](/blog/how-referral-rewards-work).

### Daily rewards and streaks
Logging in on consecutive days may unlock bigger rewards. Missing a day may reset a streak.

### Games and spins
Some platforms add games, spin wheels or giveaways. These often involve chance, so outcomes are not guaranteed.

## Points, balances and redemption

Rewards are usually counted in points or a platform currency. Read [what reward points are and how they are redeemed](/blog/what-are-reward-points) to understand conversion, minimums and expiry.

## What to check before you join

- **Clear rules.** Are reward amounts, limits and conditions written down?
- **Redemption details.** What are the minimums, timing and methods?
- **Company information.** Is there an About page, contact method, terms and privacy policy?
- **Realistic promises.** Be wary of any platform that promises guaranteed income.
- **Data requests.** Only share information that is reasonable for the activity.

See our checklist: [Are reward platforms safe?](/blog/are-reward-platforms-safe)

## A realistic expectation

Reward platforms are generally a way to receive small rewards for activities you do. Earnings depend on your activity, platform rules and availability of tasks. Nothing should be treated as guaranteed income.

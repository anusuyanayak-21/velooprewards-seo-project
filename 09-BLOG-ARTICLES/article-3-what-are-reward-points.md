## What Are Reward Points?

Reward points are units a platform gives you for taking part in activities. They are recorded in your account and can usually be used in the ways the platform allows. They are not automatically the same as money; their value and use are set by each platform.

## How are points earned?

Typical sources include watching ads, finishing tasks, referring friends, daily logins, games and promotions. Each activity has its own reward and sometimes daily limits.

## Points, tokens and other units

Platforms use different names for their units: points, coins, gems, tokens or credits. The name matters less than the rules around them:

- What is it called, and what can it be used for?
- Can one unit convert into another? At what rate?
- Is there a minimum before you can redeem?
- Does it expire?

## How redemption works

1. **Check your balance.** Usually in a wallet or account page.
2. **Open the redemption area.** Names vary (store, exchange, rewards).
3. **Choose an option.** Options may include gift items, credits, conversions or withdrawals.
4. **Meet conditions.** Common conditions are minimum balance, account verification and processing time.
5. **Confirm and wait.** Some redemptions are instant; others take time.

## Things that commonly affect redemption

- **Minimum thresholds.**
- **Verification.** You may be asked to confirm identity or contact details.
- **Processing time.**
- **Expiry or inactivity rules.**
- **Fees or conversion rates**, if any.

## Example (generic)

A platform gives 10 points per completed task and lets you redeem once you have 500 points. After 50 tasks you reach the threshold and can choose a redemption option. These numbers are only an illustration.

# VELoop Rewards - SEO Growth and Organic Traffic Project

## Project
Organic search growth project for https://www.velooprewards.in/ covering SEO audit, keyword research, competitor analysis, content strategy, on-page and technical recommendations, link building and a 3-month roadmap.

## About VELoop Rewards
A rewards and engagement platform with activities such as Watch Ads & Earn, Refer & Earn, Daily Rewards, Spin & Win, Games, Tasks, Giveaways, an Exchange Center, wallet and withdrawals. Public facts are limited; see VELOOP_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md.

## SEO Objective
Increase organic search visibility and opportunities to attract relevant users, following Search > Content > Website > Trust > Registration, without relying on paid advertising. This strategy is designed to improve visibility; it does not guarantee rankings.

## Research Methodology
Public information only. The live homepage was fetched (title, meta description and viewport were readable; body text was not returned). Other pages are listed as checks to complete. Keyword ideas come from the brief, feature analysis and search-intent reasoning. No search volumes were invented.

## Tools Used
Live page fetch and web search for initial checks. To complete: Google Search, Google Trends, Keyword Planner, PageSpeed Insights, Rich Results Test, Screaming Frog (free), Ubersuggest/Ahrefs Webmaster Tools.

## Keyword Research
Five categories (Core, Feature, Informational, Comparison, Brand) with intent, priority and target page. Volume and difficulty are blank, to be filled from a named tool.

## Competitor Research
Swagbucks, Freecash, JumpTask and Toluna. Matrix and gap report contain hypotheses marked VERIFY.

## Content Strategy
Topic clusters (Rewards Basics, Earning, Referrals, Redemption and Trust, Games), a 3-month calendar and four draft articles.

## Technical SEO Findings
Observed: HTTPS works, title and meta present, viewport present. To verify: JavaScript rendering, robots.txt, sitemap, canonicals, noindex on private pages, Core Web Vitals.

## On-Page Strategy
New homepage title/meta/H1 without unsupported claims; public feature and trust pages; private dashboard pages noindex.

## Link-Building Strategy
White-hat only: 12 PR/content opportunities, linkable assets, no purchased or automated links.

## Expected Business Impact
Better indexability, trust content and a coherent content base that can increase relevant organic visits and registrations over time. No ranking or traffic is guaranteed.

## Limitations
Only homepage metadata was readable; no Search Console, Analytics, crawl or competitor data was collected; articles and pages need fact-checking and VELoop confirmation; AI assisted drafting, so all work requires human review and editing before submission.
